# Royale Aaroma luxury storefront

## Scope
Build a polished, responsive e-commerce prototype at `/` that demonstrates the complete premium shopping journey without external checkout or account infrastructure.

## Experience
- Light quiet-luxury visual system with ivory, mineral green, muted clay, and ink tones; no black-and-gold theme.
- Classic editorial serif headlines paired with a highly legible sans-serif body face.
- Desktop sticky navigation with shop menu and utilities; mobile full-screen menu plus bottom navigation.
- Product-led homepage with an immersive first viewport, signature collection, scent-family explorer, brand story, craftsmanship, reviews, journal, and newsletter.
- Interactive product cards, quick product view, size and quantity controls, cart drawer, shipping progress, currency selection, search overlay, and mobile sticky purchase actions.
- Purposeful short animations with reduced-motion support, semantic structure, visible focus treatment, large tap targets, and optimized local imagery.

## Content and assets
- Create a cohesive editorial fragrance image suite for bottles, ingredients, and workshop storytelling.
- Use refined sample products and brand copy rooted in Indian perfumery traditions.
- Keep all product, currency, and cart data local to the prototype; payment, live inventory, accounts, language translation, and shipping APIs remain presentation-only until a commerce backend is connected.

## Validation and delivery
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts in-browser, test navigation/cart/product interactions, and check for runtime or build errors.
- Package the finished source as a downloadable ZIP archive.
